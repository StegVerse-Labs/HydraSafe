# Licensed Professional Partner Brief

## Opportunity

HydraSafe prepares structured, documentation-only permitting-readiness packets for CVD and HPHT facilities using hydrogen or other reactive gases. Some customer submissions may require review, correction, certification, or stamping by a licensed fire-protection, mechanical, chemical, or process-safety professional engineer.

HydraSafe seeks independent licensed professionals available on a per-engagement basis.

## HydraSafe provides

- organized customer-supplied records;
- document inventory and provenance;
- hazard-review framework;
- permit and evidence matrix;
- commissioning and inspection checklist frameworks;
- incident-response documentation framework;
- open-question and gap registers;
- versioned review package;
- administrative coordination and controlled revisions.

## Professional retains

The professional retains sole control over:

- whether the engagement is within their competence and license jurisdiction;
- required calculations, inspection, analysis, and supplemental evidence;
- interpretation of applicable codes and standards;
- requested corrections;
- whether any document can be signed, sealed, certified, or submitted;
- limitations and conditions attached to professional work.

HydraSafe will not represent that a stamp is guaranteed or direct the professional's judgment.

## Preferred engagement model

- Independent per-project agreement or subcontract.
- Fixed review fee where scope is known; hourly or change-order terms for unresolved conditions.
- Clear separation between HydraSafe documentation assembly and professional engineering work.
- Directly inspectable record of documents reviewed and disposition.
- License and insurance verification before assignment.

## Initial partner qualification

Requested information:

1. legal name and firm;
2. license number, discipline, status, and jurisdictions;
3. relevant hydrogen, compressed-gas, semiconductor, industrial-process, fire-code, or hazardous-material experience;
4. professional liability coverage;
5. typical review scope, fee model, and availability;
6. conflicts or geographic restrictions;
7. willingness to review a bounded sample packet before accepting customer work.

## Introductory message

Subject: Per-project PE review partnership for hydrogen permitting packets

We assemble controlled facility packets from customer records, including document inventories, hazard-review structures, commissioning and inspection checklists, incident-response frameworks, and evidence matrices.

We are seeking an independent licensed PE for per-project review where jurisdictional or customer requirements call for engineering judgment or a stamp. The professional retains full control of scope, corrections, and whether any work can be signed or sealed. HydraSafe handles document assembly and coordination; it does not perform or represent engineering approval.
