## 2. Objective

Assemble a documentation-only hydrogen permitting-readiness packet from customer-supplied records and approved HydraSafe frameworks for review by the customer, OEM, insurer, authority having jurisdiction, and qualified professionals.

## 3. Deliverables

The selected engagement may include:

1. document inventory and provenance register;
2. facility/process narrative framework;
3. hazard-review structure and open-question register;
4. permit requirement and evidence matrix;
5. commissioning checklist;
6. inspection and recurring-record checklist;
7. incident-response playbook framework;
8. external-review register;
9. consolidated gap and disposition register;
10. controlled final evidence package and export manifest.

Only selected deliverables listed in the executed proposal are included.

## 4. Customer responsibilities

The customer will:

- provide accurate, current, authorized documents;
- identify the facility owner, operator, OEM, installer, insurer, AHJ, and engineering reviewers where applicable;
- make qualified personnel available for factual clarification;
- review drafts and resolve factual inaccuracies;
- obtain all approvals, permits, stamps, inspections, and operational authorizations.

## 5. HydraSafe responsibilities

HydraSafe will:

- organize supplied evidence;
- apply versioned documentation frameworks;
- identify missing, inconsistent, stale, or unresolved documentary items;
- record provenance and review posture;
- coordinate controlled document revisions within scope;
- preserve the documentation-only authority boundary.

## 6. Exclusions

Unless separately contracted through a properly qualified and licensed party, the engagement excludes engineering design, calculations, code certification, legal advice, physical inspection, equipment installation, maintenance, operation, permit issuance, PE stamping, and authorization to start or continue operations.

## 7. External professional review

Where a licensed professional's review or stamp is required, that work will be governed by a separate agreement identifying:

- professional identity and license jurisdiction;
- professional scope and fee;
- documents reviewed;
- assumptions and requested corrections;
- final disposition and signature/stamp status.

HydraSafe does not direct or predetermine the professional's independent judgment.
