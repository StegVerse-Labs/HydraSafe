# Free Hydrogen Documentation Gap Assessment

## Purpose

Provide a bounded, no-cost review of a prospect's existing hydrogen and reactive-gas documentation to identify whether a paid HydraSafe permitting-readiness engagement is warranted.

## Included

- review of documents supplied by the prospect;
- comparison against the HydraSafe readiness checklist;
- classification of each review area as `PRESENT`, `MISSING`, `INCONSISTENT`, `STALE`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, or `SPECIALIST_REVIEW_REQUIRED`;
- one concise gap report;
- one 30-minute findings call;
- optional fixed-scope proposal for paid remediation and packet assembly.

## Excluded

The free assessment does not include:

- engineering calculations or design;
- code interpretation represented as authoritative;
- site inspection;
- permit preparation for filing;
- PE review or stamp;
- legal advice;
- insurer, AHJ, OEM, owner, installer, or operator approval;
- modification of physical systems;
- operational authorization.

## Requested customer documents

Where available:

1. facility and process narrative;
2. hydrogen and reactive-gas inventory summary;
3. process flow or gas distribution diagram;
4. equipment/OEM documentation;
5. detection, alarm, shutdown, and ventilation descriptions;
6. commissioning and inspection records;
7. preventive maintenance procedures;
8. emergency and incident-response procedures;
9. existing permit submissions, comments, or approvals;
10. insurer or AHJ correspondence relevant to the project.

The prospect may redact sensitive information. HydraSafe should request only information necessary for the assessment.

## Delivery standard

The gap report must:

- identify the facility and assessment version;
- list documents received;
- state the review boundary;
- separate documentary gaps from engineering or jurisdictional questions;
- identify all items requiring licensed-professional, OEM, insurer, or AHJ review;
- avoid declaring a facility compliant, safe, approved, or ready to operate;
- include an expiration date because facility conditions and requirements change.

## Conversion trigger

A paid proposal may be offered when one or more material gaps can be remediated through documentation assembly, evidence organization, checklist completion, controlled drafting, or external-review coordination.

Engineering work and stamping must be separately scoped to the licensed professional responsible for that work.
